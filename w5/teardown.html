  • At the foot of the page, under “*Disclaimer”: “Emotiv products are intended to be used for research applications and personal use only. The privacy policy

EMOTIV Privacy Policy, “Effective Date: August 6, 2026”. Paragraph: “Delivering Personalized Emotiv-IQ Analytics, Data Pooling, & Model Development”

When you explicitly request Emotiv-IQ, processing your pseudonymized Neural Data and aggregating and pooling it into our Large Brain Model (LBM) is necessary to generate and deliver your personalized cognitive metrics. […] Data pooling across consenting users enables the LBM to continuously improve its accuracy, calibrate population baselines, and power current and future features.

EMOTIV Privacy Policy, “Effective Date: August 6, 2026”. Same paragraph

You may disable Emotiv-IQ at any time in Account Settings. Doing so halts future ingestion of your Neural Data into LBM training. It does not remove contributions already incorporated into a trained model, because individual contributions cannot be isolated from model weights once training is complete. Where technically feasible we will apply retraining or machine unlearning techniques at the next full training cycle.

EMOTIV Privacy Policy, “Effective Date: August 6, 2026”. Section: “How and with whom we share information”

EMOTIV may license trained Large Brain Model (LBM) parameters, weights, or API outputs to commercial, enterprise, and research partners . EMOTIV does not sell raw Neural Data or Personal Information to third parties.

EMOTIV Privacy Policy, “Effective Date: August 6, 2026”. Same section

If EMOTIV is involved in a merger, consolidation, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets or other corporate transaction, EMOTIV may transfer your Personal Information as part of that transaction.

The privacy policy

INTERAXON’S PRIVACY POLICY, “Effective Date of Privacy Policy: Sept 29, 2026”. Section: “DEFINITIONS”

“Sensor Data” means data such as brainwave patterns (through electroencephalogram or EEG), […] collected through the sensors on the Muse device.

INTERAXON’S PRIVACY POLICY, “Effective Date of Privacy Policy: Sept 29, 2026”. Section: “When using the Muse mobile application”

When you use the Muse application with the Muse device, we record, process and store your Activity Data, Sensor Data, Preference Data, Processed Data and Transmission Data. We use the data referenced in the preceding sentence to provide you with […] brain/body scientific research, […] and the improvement of our own algorithms and machine-learning models, including foundation models, that power Muse features such as sleep staging and brain-health metrics. Data used for this purpose is first de-identified.

INTERAXON’S PRIVACY POLICY, “Effective Date of Privacy Policy: Sept 29, 2026”. Section: “Muse Connect Web Application”

The Muse Connect web application is designed to allow a party (such as a coach, wellness professional or employer participating in a wellness program) (the “Observer”) to monitor, from the Muse Connect dashboard, the Muse sessions of one or more people using Muse devices in conjunction with the Muse Mobile application (the “Participant(s)”). […] We do not, however, control the Observer’s use of that data; we therefore recommend that, if you wish to be a Participant, you review the privacy policy of the Observer.

INTERAXON’S PRIVACY POLICY, “Effective Date of Privacy Policy: Sept 29, 2026”. Section: “BUSINESS TRANSACTIONS”

We may transfer personal information as an asset in connection with a proposed or completed merger or sale (including transfers made as part of insolvency or bankruptcy proceeding) involving all or part of our group of companies or as part of a corporate reorganization, financing, or other change in corporate control.
